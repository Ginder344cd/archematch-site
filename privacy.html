Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

ArcheMatch, Inc. ("ArcheMatch," "we," "us") operates archematch.com and the Rainmaker platform at rainmakerapp.io. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide. When you contact us or create a Rainmaker account, we collect the information you submit — such as your name, email address, firm, LinkedIn profile URL, and your responses to the archetype assessment.
  • Usage information. We use analytics tools (including Google Analytics and AdvizorPro) that collect standard usage data such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and referral source, typically via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Prospect research inputs. If you use Rainmaker, the platform processes information you supply about prospects and clients, together with publicly available professional information, solely to deliver the service to you.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve ArcheMatch and Rainmaker.
  • To generate your archetype profile, compatibility analysis, and coaching responses.
  • To respond to your inquiries and send service-related communications.
  • To understand how our site and product are used so we can make them better.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your assessment results or your prospect research with other customers.
  • We do not use your client or prospect data to train models for anyone else's benefit.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate business purposes, and we use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us at info@archematch.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

Children

Our services are intended for financial professionals and are not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions? Email info@archematch.com.
